The CFOP method

Four steps, solved in order. Most of your speed comes from looking ahead so there are no pauses between them.

Cross

Plan in inspection

Build a cross on the bottom face, with each edge matching its center. Plan all of it before the timer starts, then keep the cross on the bottom for the rest of the solve.

F2L

First two layers

Insert each corner and its edge together as a pair, four times. This is about two thirds of your solve, so it's where practice pays off most. Learn to find the next pair while you insert the current one.

OLL

Orient last layer

Make the whole top face one color. Beginners can use a 2-look approach (edges, then corners) and later learn all 57 one-step algorithms below.

PLL

Permute last layer

Move the top pieces into place to finish. 2-look PLL needs 6 algorithms. Full PLL is 21, listed below.

Beginner method

Solve the cube one layer at a time. It takes more moves than CFOP, but it only needs 7 short algorithms and no memorizing of big lists. When it feels easy, jump to CFOP.

How to read the moves

Each letter is a face: U up, D down, F front, R right, L left. A plain letter turns that face a quarter turn clockwise, looking straight at it. A ' (like R') turns it the other way. A 2 (like U2) is a half turn.
